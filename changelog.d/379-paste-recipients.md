**A list of addresses pasted into To, Cc or Bcc becomes one recipient per address.** Separated by
semicolons, commas or new lines, with or without names, as Outlook and Gmail copy them: `jo@jo.fr;
Ann <ann@example.org>`. An address already in the field is not added twice, and what is not an
address stays in the box with the usual error. No schema change.
[#379](https://github.com/karatektus/pl_mail/issues/379), by @jo2904.
